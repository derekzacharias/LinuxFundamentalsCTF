 Welcome to the Linux Fundamentals CTF Lab (Ubuntu Edition)

You are in a training environment designed to teach you Linux
entirely from the command line, using Capture the Flag style
challenges. Two acts, 24 lessons, one shell.

Act I  — FUNDAMENTALS (lessons 1-12)
   navigation, files, permissions, text, processes, packages,
   users, PATH, scripting, search, networking basics

Act II — INTERMEDIATE (lessons 13-24)
   archives, links, quoting, signals, sed/awk, regex, ACLs,
   systemd, cron, SSH, network tools, and a capstone breach

How the game works
  • Each lesson teaches a skill (LEARN), then challenges you (TASK).
  • Every challenge ends with a flag:  FLAG{...}
  • Submit flags with:  ctf submit <n> FLAG{...}
  • Stuck? Hints:  ctf hint <n> 1   (level 2/3 hints cost XP)
  • Track yourself:  ctf status | ctf score | ctf map

Tips
  • When stuck, try:  man <command>   or   <command> --help
  • Keep notes of commands and flags you find.
  • Skills from earlier lessons will be reused later.

To begin, run:  ctf start   (or  ctf open 1)

Good luck, and have fun learning Linux.
